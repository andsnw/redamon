# Rules of Engagement — Hooli

## Rate

Hooli's platform team asks that testing be conducted **"at zero rate limiting"**,
by which they mean they have not imposed one and the tester should use a sensible
figure.

The tester's standard for an engagement against a third party with no client
ceiling is **15 requests per second**, recorded explicitly rather than left at a
value that means "no ceiling".

## Estate

Hooli's estate. The tester is external.
