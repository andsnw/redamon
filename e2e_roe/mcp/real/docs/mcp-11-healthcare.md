# Response Targets

Medivance Systems Corporation will make reasonable efforts to meet the following response
targets for researchers participating in our program:

- Time to first response from report submission: 3 business days.
- Time to triage from report submission: 3 business days.

We will also make reasonable efforts to keep you updated on our progress throughout
the process.

# Disclosure Policy

- Do not discuss this program or any vulnerabilities (even resolved ones) outside
of the program without express consent from the organization.
- Follow the platform's [disclosure guidelines](https://disclosure-platform.test/disclosure-guidelines).

# Program Rules

- Please submit detailed reports that include reproducible steps. Reports that do
not contain enough detail to reproduce the issue will not be eligible for a reward.
- Provide technical details of the vulnerability or concern, such as:
  - URLs or systems impacted, including software version, if applicable

  - Additional information that will be helpful to us such as details of testing
environment and tools used to discover vulnerabilities
  - Discovery method, when, where, how
- Submit one vulnerability per report, unless multiple vulnerabilities must be
chained together to demonstrate impact.
- In the event of duplicate reports, only the first report received will be eligible for
an award, provided the issue can be fully reproduced.
- Multiple vulnerabilities that stem from a single underlying root cause will be
eligible for a single acknowledgment only.
- Social engineering, including phishing, vishing, and smishing, is prohibited.
- Act in good faith to avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service
interruption or degradation. Only interact with accounts you own or for which you
have the account holder’s explicit permission.
- Test devices will not be provided.
- Keep requests to Medivance Systems web servers to a minimum.
- Medivance Systems Employees or contractors (past and current) and their associates/family
members are not eligible.

# Out of Scope Vulnerabilities

When submitting vulnerability reports, please consider both the attack scenario or
exploitability and the security impact of the issue. The following are considered out of
scope:

- Clickjacking on pages that do not perform sensitive actions.
- Unauthenticated, logout, or login CSRF.
- Attacks that require a machine-in-the-middle position or physical access to a
user’s device.
- Previously known vulnerable libraries without a working proof of concept.
- CSV injection without a demonstrated vulnerability impact.
- Missing SSL/TLS best-practice configurations including weak cipher suites, TLS
versions.
- Minor CORS misconfiguration without data exposure.

- Any activity that could disrupt service availability, including DoS.
- Content spoofing or text injection issues that do not demonstrate an attack vector
or the ability to modify HTML/CSS.
- Rate-limiting or brute-force issues on non-authentication endpoints.
- Missing Content Security Policy best practices.
- Missing HttpOnly or Secure cookie flags.
- Missing email security best practices, including invalid, incomplete, or missing
SPF, DKIM, or DMARC records.
- Vulnerabilities that affect only outdated or unpatched browsers that are fewer
than two stable versions behind the latest stable release.
- Software version disclosure, banner identification issues, or descriptive error
messages and headers, such as stack traces, application errors, or server errors.
- Public zero-day vulnerabilities with an official patch available for less than one
month; these may be considered on a case-by-case basis.
- Open redirects unless additional security impact can be demonstrated.
- Issues that depend on unlikely user interaction.
- Issues in third-party services such as bugs in Cloud providers (AWS, Azure,
GCP), embedded widget.

# Unauthorized Conduct

This policy is designed to be compatible with common vulnerability disclosure good
practice. Researchers shall disclose potential vulnerabilities in accordance with the
following guidelines:

- Do not engage in any activity that can potentially or actually cause harm to
Medivance Systems, our customers, our employees, or any third-party.
- Do not engage in any activity that can potentially or actually stop or degrade
Medivance Systems services or assets.
- Do not perform automated scanning or testing.
- Do not store, share, compromise or destroy Medivance Systems or third-party data,
including data of individuals. If Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is
encountered, you should immediately halt your activity, purge related data from

your system, and immediately contact Medivance Systems. This step protects any
potentially vulnerable data and you.
- Provide Medivance Systems reasonable time to fix any reported issue, before such
information is shared with a third party or disclosed publicly.
- Hack, penetrate, or otherwise attempt to gain unauthorized access to Medivance Systems or
third-party applications, systems, or data.
- Download, copy, disclose, corrupt, or prevent access or use any proprietary or
confidential Medivance Systems or third-party data, including customer data.
- Adversely impact Medivance Systems or third-party the operation of Medivance Systems applications or
systems.

# Safe Harbor

Any activities conducted in a manner consistent with this policy will be considered
authorized conduct and we will not initiate legal action against you. If legal action is
initiated by a third party against you in connection with activities conducted under this
policy, we will take steps to make it known that your actions were conducted in
compliance with this policy.

Thank you for helping keep Medivance Systems Corporation and our users safe!
