# Introduction

Welcome to the Cascade Registry Vulnerability Disclosure Program!

At Cascade Registry we take security seriously. If you believe that you have found a security vulnerability in any of the systems in scope, please report it to us using the "Submit Report" button on this page.

Cascade Registry appreciates your participation in this program and looks forward to your findings.

# Disclosure Policy

Please follow the platform's [Vulnerability Disclosure Guidelines](https://disclosure-platform.test/terms/disclosure-guidelines)

# Unqualified Issues

Please refer to the  [Core Ineligible Findings](https://docs.disclosure-platform.test/en/articles/8494488-core-ineligible-findings) for a list of reports we do not accept.

## Subdomains under cascade-registry.test (*.cascade-registry.test)
 
At this point we do not accept reports for subdomains under cascade-registry.test (*.cascade-registry.test). This includes but is not limited to subdomain takeovers, directory listings, wordpress installs, publicly accessible software archives.

## Support websites

We do not take reports for our support sites. These websites are hosted by a third party:

* support.cascade-registry.test
* support.cascade-registry.test
* support.cascade-registry.test
* support.cascade-registry.test
* support.cascade-registry.test

Please redirect reports for these sites to the H1 program for the vendor at https://disclosure-platform.test/freshworks

## InfoStealer, Third-party breaches, Malware Stealer Credentials 
Reports involving credentials harvested via malware, phishing, or third-party breaches are out of scope and not eligible for reward.
While these findings are not in scope for this program, we take credential leaks and data exposure seriously. If you do discover such information, please share it with us as informational— it helps inform our internal monitoring and remediation processes.

# Safe Harbour

Cascade Registry supports a transparent, respectful, and collaborative approach to vulnerability disclosure. If you follow this policy in good faith, we consider your security research authorized and will not take legal action.


Thank you for helping keep Cascade Registry and our users safe!
